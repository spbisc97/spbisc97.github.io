What is BrainJuice?

BrainJuice is a small group for conversations on intelligence—how machines learn, how humans reason, and how the two intersect. We share experiments, reading lists, and practical prompts for builders and researchers.

Topics we explore

  • Robotics, control, and embodied intelligence.
  • Cognition, perception, and learning systems.
  • Philosophy of mind and human-computer collaboration.
  • Tools, simulations, and hands-on experiments.

Formats

  • Meetups

    Small in-person sessions around robotics labs, cafés, or studios.

  • Online calls

    Lightweight virtual discussions to share experiments and readings.

  • Content

    LinkedIn and Instagram for short thoughts, links, and prompts.
